FIND YOUR OFFER

Stuck on "I do not know what to sell"? This digs it out of you.

It runs inside Claude and interviews you the way Alex Hormozi would, using his three Ps:
  Pain        a problem you had and fixed for yourself
  Profession  a job you were paid to do
  Passion     a thing you do when nobody is paying you

It asks about your life, your wins and who you know. It pushes when you give a soft answer.
Then it flips and asks who would pay.

About 24 questions. 15 to 20 minutes. You can stop and come back.
Starting from zero? Run it anyway. You still leave with a plan.


HOW TO INSTALL (pick one)

1. Claude Code, the easy way
   Download find-your-offer.zip. Open Claude Code and paste this:

   Unzip find-your-offer.zip from my Downloads folder and put the find-your-offer folder inside ~/.claude/skills/

   Open a new Claude Code session and type /find-your-offer

2. Claude Code, by hand
   Unzip find-your-offer.zip. Drag the find-your-offer folder into ~/.claude/skills/
   Mac: in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste ~/.claude/skills
   Windows: C:\Users\<you>\.claude\skills\
   Open a new Claude Code session and type /find-your-offer

3. Claude.ai, no Claude Code
   Make a new Project. Upload SKILL.md, interview.md and routes.md from the
   find-your-offer folder as project knowledge. Type: find your offer
   Claude cannot save files there, so copy your result page when it prints.


HOW TO USE IT

/find-your-offer          starts it, or picks up where you stopped
/find-your-offer result   shows your result again
/find-your-offer reset    starts over

Answer one question at a time. Short and true beats long and polished.
Some questions are open. Some give you options: type the letter, or say it your own way.
If you say "I am good with people" it will ask for one real time it happened. Give it one.
When it asks for names, give real names. A type of person is not a name.


WHAT YOU GET AT THE END

- Your unique talents
- The gap in the market you can fill
- The skills you already have
- Your route, with a rough offer in one sentence
- Your research plan: the podcast method, or the white paper method if you
  will not go on camera
- The invite to send, the six questions to ask, the people to start with
- A one-page version that opens in your browser


THE THREE ROUTES

A. You have an edge.       A service built on your own talent.
B. No clear edge yet.      The simplest model (lead generation, or AI setup
                           if you are good with tech), aimed at people you know.
C. Starting from zero.     You pick one of five niches and start there.

Every route ends the same way: go and talk to buyers, write down the problems
you keep hearing, and build your offer from those.


WHAT IT WILL NOT DO

It never makes up a skill, a result or a number for you.
It never tells you what you will earn or what to charge.
It never sends anything for you.
It does not build your full offer. The market does that.
